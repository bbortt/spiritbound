**Title**
Death destroys gear and un-attuned cards; only attuned cards, within budget, survive

**Lens**: SYS

**Status**: active

**Description**
When a character dies, all equipped gear and item instances are destroyed
unconditionally, and only cards the player had explicitly attuned
beforehand (within their spirit's rarity-tiered slot budget) survive into
the next life — everything else, including every card if the account has
no personal spirit at all, is lost.

**Rationale**
Per `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`/`site/mechanics.md`: "your gear is always lost,
no exceptions" (the permanent economy sink) and "your cards are a choice,
not a coin flip" (attunement is decided ahead of time, not rolled).
