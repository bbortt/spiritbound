**Title**
The Hand is a spirit-level-gated card loadout, changed through equip/unequip

**Lens**: SYS

**Status**: active

**Description**
A character's Hand (currently-castable active cards plus always-on
passive cards) has separate slot caps that grow with the account's
personal spirit's level, not the character's own level — equipping and
unequipping cards into it is how a player commits to a build.

**Rationale**
"You can only change your Hand at a spirit... you commit to a build
before you go looking for trouble" (`site/mechanics.md`) — the Hand is
deliberately a between-fights decision, not a mid-combat option.
