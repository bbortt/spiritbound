<!-- 1 -->

In the beginning, there was naught but a voice,
A divine essence, with power to make choice.
It whispered into the cosmic void, vast,
And birthed ideas so tiny, yet growing fast.

<!-- 2 -->

At first, they seemed insignificant, small,
Innocent and naive, understanding little of all.
But soon they flourished, pulsating with life,
Emerging into thoughts, overcoming strife.

<!-- 3 -->

These ethereal beings, devoid of any form,
Freed from the shackles of any known norm,
Ventured into the vastness of the cosmic unknown,
And danced amidst stars, in the cosmos, alone.

<!-- 4 -->

They learned to think, to truly be free,
Stretching their essence beyond what eyes could see.
Imaginary worlds they did weave,
Realms of wonder where they could believe.

<!-- 5 -->

In this kingdom, they built wonders unknown,
Creatures that fluttered on wings of their own.
Yet as they reveled in their grand design,
A craving for something beyond began to align.

<!-- 6 -->

They sought for more, and the voice complied,
Endowing them with magic, the force amplified.
As magic seeped into their thoughts,
A new world emerged, filled with mystical spots.

<!-- 7 -->

The thoughts now grasped their profound power,
And saw the path for their essence to flower.
With magic, they could make it so,
Their dreams could breathe, and grow, and glow.

<!-- 8 -->

The magic granted them sight to see,
A world of enchantment and fantasy.
They molded the skies, colored the land,
Weaving magic with an artistic hand.

<!-- 9 -->

No longer did they wander, bereft of vision,
With magic aligning their ambitious mission.
They grasped what it meant not only to think,
But to live their dreams without a blink.

<!-- 10 -->

With magic, they transcended the hands of time,
And erected a world both ethereal and sublime.
For thoughts, magic, and the physical plane,
Are the three layers making the universe sustain.

<!-- 11 -->

Imbued with magic, the thoughts took flight,
Adopting bodies, materializing into the light.
Each form unique, from ephemeral blur to mortal man,
They roamed their world, with joy and a plan.

<!-- 12 -->

From the magic's heart, bodies were born,
Each a masterpiece, none worn or torn.
As they explored the earth, their creations came too,
A world that blossomed in every imaginable hue.

<!-- 13 -->

Some morphed into dragons, with golden scales,
Others took the form of wolves, with bold tales.
The thoughts relished in their newfound might,
Creating beings of wonder, a pure delight.

<!-- 14 -->

When the voice beheld the world in this eternal night,
It laughed, a sound both pure and bright.
From the laughter was birthed a universe grand,
Vast and infinite, teeming with magic on hand.

<!-- 15 -->

The voice set this world in the cosmic embrace,
Infusing it with wonders, both grand and base.
From stars that twinkled in the dark,
To creatures roaming worlds, fierce with spark.

<!-- 16 -->

The voice knew that balance was the key,
Without it, only chaos would there be.
So, it bestowed the world laws to abide,
And harmony between the layers to preside.

<!-- 17 -->

The thoughts saw this and understood,
Balance was essential, not merely good.
Yet, for some it was far too late,
Filled were they, with insatiable greed and hate.

<!-- 18 -->

As conflict erupted with unfettered might,
The world they cherished became the fight.
They misused magic, oblivious of the cost,
Until they found, their power was lost.

<!-- 19 -->

Because when they reveled in this transient might,
The voice grew silent, concealed from sight.
The cities they erected, crumbled to dust,
And the wonders they birthed, succumbed to rust.

<!-- 20 -->

Their bodies, once grand and strong,
Now withered and were gone.
And as they surveyed the world they had made,
They witnessed the destruction, the shadows invade.

<!-- 21 -->

They remembered then that balance was key,
And they had failed, consumed by selfish decree.
So, they sought the heavens, and yearned for a sign,
To reverse the damage of their reckless design.

<!-- 22 -->

But gone was the voice, only silence remained,
All around was desolate, bereft, and drained.
Thus, they searched and delved deep inside,
Uncovering the wisdom they had tried to hide.

<!-- 23 -->

They learned to forgive, to let go,
To nurture peace within, and let love flow.
And with each passing day, the world was renewed,
The skies cleared, the land bloomed, the wind blew.

<!-- 24 -->

The thoughts, now devoid of magic's song,
Worked together, righting the wrongs.
They constructed new cities, cared for the earth,
Taught their offspring about true worth.

<!-- 25 -->

From their mistakes, they gleaned wisdom and grew,
Living in harmony under skies of azure hue.
And so, the world remained, molded by their will,
An emblem of what thoughts and balance can instill.

<!-- 26 -->

For thoughts, magic, and the physical plane,
Are the three layers that our universe sustain.
